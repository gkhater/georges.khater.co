How hard is it
to decode reality?

in brief

With Professor Louay Bazzi, I study the computational complexity of minimum-weight decoding for two-dimensional topological quantum codes.

syndrome → minimum weight → hard

01 / the paper

Hardness of Approximation for Minimum-Weight Decoding of Two-Dimensional Topological Quantum Codes establishes polynomial additive inapproximability gaps for several topological-code decoding problems, assuming P ≠ NP.

Read on arXiv ↗

02 / in my own words

This project started with us trying to find algorithms for decoding, which meant getting stumped by NP-hardness first hand. What I like most is that, once you abstract away the quantum stuff, you are left with a really cool graph-theory problem. Bonus points because it has a practical application.
